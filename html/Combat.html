Combat

About Combat

Although it has been highly advised NOT to fight with the dangerous creatures. If violence continues, then we will eventually have to negotiated with fists and bullets.

Related Attributes

There are various attributes of investigator that may affect the combat:

  • DEX: decide your action order in the battle.
  • DB: base on STR and SIZ, it means the extra damage you can make in melee combat
  • Build: base on STR and SIZ, affect the success ratio of using maneuvers
  • Combat Skills: the skill points of skills like Brawl, Firearms, Melee Weapeons
  • Dodge: the skill points of dodge decide the success ratio for dodging enermy's attack.

Combat Rules

The logic of Combat round is similar to Opposed Rolls. It basically just a set of Opposed Rolls which take turns by DEXand the type of skills that can be used is limited. Everyone will attack once(mostly) in a round and the target use a skill to counter or dodge. The Combat will continues until one side leave or die.

The most important difference of Opposed Rolls in Combat and the normal situation is the process of tie. In Combat Round, the win and lose when the success level is tie follow the rules:

Dodge ＞ Attack (Brawl, Firearms, Magic...) ＞ Counter (Brawl, Firearms, Magic...)

More details can be find in the Offical Rulebook.
